What We Do

Our Core Services

From initial design and calculations to construction support and existing-building assessments, we provide practical electrical engineering solutions for new and existing buildings.

MEP Project Management

Manage complex MEP projects from planning and coordination through construction, including consultant and contractor coordination, schedule and budget tracking, technical review, site coordination, and project delivery.

Electrical Load & Power Calculations

Evaluate electrical loads and power requirements for properly sizing services, feeders, distribution equipment, and electrical systems in accordance with applicable codes and standards.

Design & Drawings

Develop detailed electrical plans, schematics, and layouts for new construction and renovation projects, including power distribution, equipment connections, and electrical infrastructure.

Code Compliance & Engineering Review

Review existing and new electrical systems and designs assess existing electrical systems, identify deficiencies, for code compliance and identify required corrections.

Lighting Design

Design practical and efficient lighting systems tailored to the building’s use, space requirements, energy objectives, and applicable codes and standards.

Construction Support, Audits, and Assessments

Conduct site inspections, provide office-based supervision and shop drawing reviews, and support construction through design updates, and technical reporting.
