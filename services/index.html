Full-Lifecycle Engineering

What We Deliver

From concept to completion, providing electrical engineering and MEP project services including design, calculations, BIM coordination, code compliance, construction administration, and project management.

MEP Project Management

Manage complex MEP projects from planning and coordination through construction, including consultant and contractor coordination, schedule and budget tracking, technical review, site coordination, and project delivery.

  • Bid Coordination & Evaluation
  • Engineering Plans & Documentation
  • Project Planning & Scheduling
  • Consultant & Contractor Coordination
  • Technical Design & Document Review
  • Budget & Cost Tracking
  • Site Coordination & Construction Support

Electrical Load & Power Calculations

Evaluate electrical loads and power requirements for properly sizing services, feeders, distribution equipment, and electrical systems in accordance with applicable codes and standards.

  • Service Load Calculations
  • Feeder & Circuit Load Calculations
  • Transformer Sizing
  • Main Distribution Sizing
  • Equipment Load Calculations
  • Electrical Capacity Assessment
  • Code-Compliant Load Calculations
  • Coordination with Head Office & Service Providers (Hydro-Quebec or Others)

Design & Drawings

Develop detailed electrical plans, schematics, and layouts for new construction and renovation projects, including power distribution, equipment connections, and electrical infrastructure.

  • Electrical Power Distribution Plans
  • Lighting & Controls Plans
  • Single-Line & Riser Diagrams
  • Electrical Room Layouts
  • Panel & Distribution Schedules
  • Electrical Details & Schematics
  • BIM & Revit Coordination
  • AutoCAD, and AutoCAD Electrical
  • Multidisciplinary Design Coordination

Code Compliance & Engineering Review

Review existing and new electrical systems and designs assess existing electrical systems, identify deficiencies, for code compliance and identify required corrections.

  • New & Existing System Reviews
  • Electrical Code Compliance
  • Deficiency Identification
  • Corrective Measures & Recommendations
  • Technical Compliance Reports
  • Code & Standards Verification

Lighting Design

Design practical and efficient lighting systems tailored to the building’s use, space requirements, energy objectives, and applicable codes and standards.

  • Interior & Exterior Lighting Design
  • Lighting Layouts & Plans
  • Lighting Fixture Selection
  • Lighting Levels & Performance
  • BIM & Revit Lighting Coordination

Construction Support, Audits, and Assessments

Conduct site inspections, provide office-based supervision and shop drawing reviews, and support construction through design updates, and technical reporting.

  • Construction Site Visits
  • Field Reviews & Design Conformance
  • Shop Drawing Reviews
  • RFI & Technical Query Review
  • Change Order & Change Request Review
  • Design Updates & Revisions
  • Contractor & Consultant Coordination
  • Site Reports & Documentation
